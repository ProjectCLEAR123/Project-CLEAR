ABOUT PROJECT CLEAR

Helping students become informed digital citizens

Project CLEAR gives students the skills needed to question information, recognize misinformation, and make thoughtful decisions online.

OUR STORY

Media literacy matters more than ever

Students encounter hundreds of messages every day through social media, news websites, videos, advertisements, and online conversations. It can be difficult to determine which information is accurate and which information is misleading.

Project CLEAR was created to make media literacy approachable, useful, and relevant for young people. Our lessons help students slow down, ask questions, check evidence, and understand how media can influence their beliefs.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our mission and values

01

Critical Thinking

We encourage students to question claims and look beyond the first piece of information they see.

02

Accessibility

Our lessons are designed to be understandable, engaging, and useful for students with different learning needs.

03

Responsibility

We teach students to think carefully before believing or sharing information with others.

OUR VISION

A generation of students who can confidently navigate the digital world

We imagine a future where every student has the tools to recognize misinformation, understand different perspectives, and participate responsibly in online communities.
