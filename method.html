THE CLEAR METHOD

Five steps for evaluating information

The CLEAR Method gives students a simple process they can use before believing, reacting to, or sharing information.

HOW IT WORKS

Pause, investigate, and think clearly

C

STEP ONE

Consider the claim

Identify exactly what the post, article, video, or person is asking you to believe.

L

STEP TWO

Look at the source

Find out who created the information and whether that person or organization is qualified and trustworthy.

E

STEP THREE

Examine the evidence

Check whether the claim includes reliable facts, research, examples, quotations, or other supporting evidence.

A

STEP FOUR

Ask what is missing

Think about missing context, opposing viewpoints, emotional language, and information that may have been left out.

R

STEP FIVE

Reflect before sharing

Decide whether the information is accurate, useful, and responsible to share with someone else.

REMEMBER

You do not have to react immediately

Taking a few extra moments to verify information can prevent misinformation from spreading.
