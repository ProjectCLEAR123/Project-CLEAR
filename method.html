THE CLEAR METHOD

Five steps for evaluating information

The CLEAR Method gives students a simple process they can use before believing, reacting to, or sharing information.

HOW IT WORKS

Pause, investigate, and think clearly

C

STEP ONE

Check sources

Identify who created the information and consider whether the source is credible, trustworthy, and qualified to speak on the topic.

L

STEP TWO

Look for evidence

Check whether the claim is supported by reliable facts, research, examples, data, or other trustworthy evidence.

E

STEP THREE

Evaluate language

Pay attention to the words being used. Look for emotional, exaggerated, biased, or misleading language that may influence how you interpret the information.

A

STEP FOUR

Ask if it’s current + ask experts

Check when the information was published or updated, and consider what qualified experts or reliable organizations say about the topic.

R

STEP FIVE

Reflect before sharing

Think carefully about whether the information is accurate, responsible, and worth sharing before passing it along to others.

REMEMBER

You do not have to react immediately

Taking a few extra moments to verify information can prevent misinformation from spreading.
