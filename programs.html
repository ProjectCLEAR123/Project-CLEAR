OUR PROGRAMS

Media-literacy education designed for students

Our programs give students practical opportunities to evaluate sources, recognize misleading content, and discuss the responsibilities of digital citizenship.

WHAT WE OFFER

Programs for schools and communities

01

Student Workshops

Interactive workshops teach students how to analyze online information using realistic examples and group activities.

  • Source evaluation
  • Misinformation identification
  • Responsible sharing
02

School Partnerships

We work with schools to create media-literacy lessons that fit existing classes, student needs, and school goals.

  • Custom lesson planning
  • Classroom activities
  • Teacher support
03

Digital Resources

Students and educators can access guides, worksheets, discussion prompts, and activities for continued learning.

  • Printable worksheets
  • Source-checking guides
  • Discussion questions

FOR EDUCATORS

Flexible materials for the classroom

Project CLEAR resources can be used in English, history, science, journalism, advisory, or digital-citizenship courses. Lessons can be taught individually or combined into a larger media-literacy unit.
