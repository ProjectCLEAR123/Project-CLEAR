Who We Are

Meet the team behind Project CLEAR

We are a student-led team committed to helping young people recognize misinformation, evaluate sources, and participate responsibly in today’s digital world.

Our Team

Young leaders creating meaningful change

Project CLEAR brings together students who care about media literacy, education, research, and community engagement. Each team member contributes a different perspective while working toward the same goal: giving students practical tools to navigate information with confidence.

Leadership

Meet our founders and directors

01

Calista Chun

Founder & Board President

Calista is a high school student with interests in mathematics, history, Model United Nations, and varsity tennis. Her experience navigating social media inspired her to create Project CLEAR and help students learn how to separate fact from opinion. She believes media literacy is an essential skill for participating responsibly in today’s society.

02

Naomi Dimalanta

Executive Director

Naomi is a high school student involved in Model United Nations who also enjoys creative writing and music. She is passionate about helping students understand how to evaluate sources and identify fact-based information. Through Project CLEAR, she works to make media-literacy education practical and accessible.

Executive Team

Research, outreach, and education

03

Vivian Bullock

Director of Government Relations

Vivian is interested in English, history, Model United Nations, water polo, and the outdoors. She cares about preventing the spread of misinformation because inaccurate information can cause harm to individuals and communities. She believes reliable information is necessary for informed decision-making and public trust.

04

Sienna Chun

Social Media Manager & Science Research Intern

Sienna researches health-related topics, reviews peer-reviewed studies, and evaluates the credibility of sources shared online. She works to ensure that Project CLEAR’s educational content is supported by reliable scientific evidence and presented in a way that younger students can easily understand.
