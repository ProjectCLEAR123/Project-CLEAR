WHY IT MATTERS

Students need skills, not just warnings

Simply telling students that misinformation exists is not enough. Students need a process they can practice and remember when they encounter questionable content.

Through guided examples and discussion, students learn to identify credible sources, examine evidence, recognize emotional language, and reflect before sharing.
